# Hero video storyboard — AigenLabs Business OS

## Approval scope

Founder approved local execution for this demo asset:

- Open/use AigenLabs local app with current config.
- Use safe demo content only.
- Simulate/represent an approval gate so viewers understand founder approval exists.
- Record and create local video/poster assets for landing review.

Out of scope unless separately approved:

- Publish/deploy the landing page.
- Send outreach or messages.
- Spend money or launch ads.
- Install/connect new connectors or MCPs.
- Expose secrets, tokens, customer data, or PII.

## Scenario title

**Founder launch request → AI Agent workflow → approval → COO report**

## Business scenario

A startup founder wants to prepare AigenLabs go-to-market content safely. Instead of asking a generic chatbot, the founder asks the Business OS to coordinate AI Agent through a controlled workflow.

Demo task shown in the app:

```text
Chuẩn bị chiến dịch nội dung tuần này cho AigenLabs Business OS,
tổng hợp rủi ro trước khi publish, và gửi COO report cho founder phê duyệt.
```

## Why this scenario fits the landing hero

It proves the core positioning in one loop:

1. A business goal enters the system.
2. The goal becomes a workflow.
3. AI Agent take role-based work.
4. Execution is visible on a board.
5. Approval exists before public/customer-facing output.
6. The founder receives a COO-style report, not just a chat answer.

## Intended viewer takeaway

```text
AigenLabs is not only a chatbot.
It is a Business OS where AI Agent execute real workflows with approval and reporting.
```

## Video style

- Product cinematic, not long walkthrough.
- 16:9, suitable for the current landing hero frame.
- Autoplay muted loop friendly.
- Minimal text overlays.
- No voiceover for first draft.
- Use app UI and current config where safe.

## Safety checklist before recording

- Avoid settings pages containing API keys/tokens.
- Avoid connector setup screens showing secrets.
- Avoid real customer/prospect data.
- Use demo/local approval only.
- Do not trigger real publish, send, spend, install, or connector actions.
